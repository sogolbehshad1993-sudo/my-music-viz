# Preferences

The designer's taste and working preferences. Read at every session start; keep it under 60 lines.

## Working style

- The designer communicates through talk and sketches and walks through each piece step by step.
- Build exactly what is described, then expose likely alternatives in the panel to explore.
- Ask with AskUserQuestion whenever there are several paths, showing the options.
- Commit every step so anything can be reverted.
- Very simple code: small files, SOLID, KISS, DRY.
- No em dashes in anything written.

## Output

- Desktop only, no mobile optimization. Each experiment decides where it is shown: web, wall projection or curtain projection.
- Real time only. Even a fixed animation is shown live on the web; no video export unless explicitly asked.

## Taste

- She keeps her palette B and Love.32 colours exactly; never change them.
- Motion: slow, organised, mathematically clean.
- Points: fine, dense, small; not squares.
